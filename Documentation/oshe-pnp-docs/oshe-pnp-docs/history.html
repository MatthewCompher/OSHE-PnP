Project History

The OSHE Pick and Place is built one version per academic semester. Each version is still buildable, but V3 is the recommended design. Files for older versions are in CAD/V1-Legacy, CAD/V2-Legacy and BOMs/Legacy.

V1: Proof of concept (Fall 2025)

The first version converted an Ender 3 frame into a basic XYZ machine, running modified Marlin firmware on the Ender 3's own control board (V4.2.2). The firmware is kept in Software/Legacy-Marlin.

Machine size486 × 365 × 320 mm
Max PCB area125 × 125 mm
X-Y travel237 × 287 mm
Reel space200 mm

V2: Klipper and a rotating toolhead (Spring 2026)

V2 rebuilt most of the machine:

  • A nearly fully 3D-printed toolhead with nozzle rotation (bevel gears, thrust bearings, spring-loaded nozzle).
  • A vacuum system: 12 V diaphragm pump and normally-closed solenoid, in their own enclosure.
  • A modular printed frame (top, middle, bottom leg pieces) with roller-based X and Y axes.
  • A spring-loaded PCB clamp on rods.
  • Electronics moved to a BTT Octopus Pro with TMC5160 drivers and a Raspberry Pi 5, in a printed enclosure with an e-stop.
  • Firmware moved to Klipper with Mainsail, with sensorless homing and pick/place macros.
Machine size370 × 480 × 352.5 mm
Max PCB area250 × 250 mm
X-Y travel305 × 289 mm
Reel space280 mm

V2 test results

In testing, about 88 of 100 picks succeeded and 18 of 25 placements succeeded. Most placement failures came from one batch caused by user error, and one error tended to cause the next. The modified PushPull feeder proved unreliable on 40 mm extrusion.

The full V2 build guide, wiring and bill of materials are in the V2 documentation.

Archived experiments

  • Gerber-to-GCode (Archive/Gerber to PCB Vibecode Mini Project): a browser tool from V1 that converted Gerber files to G-code. Replaced by pnpslice.
  • USB camera calibration (Archive/USB Camera Calibration Vibecode Project): lens calibration capture and the undistorted stream service. Still used by V3.
