G-code Macros

Macros are custom commands defined in Klipper's config. Type them in the Mainsail console, click them in Mainsail's macro panel, or tap them on the touchscreen. They live in Software/Klipper/printer.cfg and Software/Klipper/pnp_hmi.cfg.

Before you jog: Z = 0 is the top and a larger Z is lower. The tallest fixtures on the plate are the reject bin (15 mm), the datum (11 mm) and the PCB clamps (8–10 mm). When jogging X/Y by hand, keep the nozzle at Z 30 or higher (a smaller number).

Homing

G28 is replaced by a [homing_override]. Plain G28 homes everything; G28 X, G28 Y, G28 Z or G28 R home only those axes. The order is:

  1. Z first, then moves to Z 5 (near the top) for clearance.
  2. Sets X and Y motor current for homing. On a CoreXY both motors move for either axis, so both are set.
  3. Y, then backs off to Y 10.
  4. X, then backs off to X 10.
  5. Restores the normal motor current.
  6. R (nozzle rotation): there's no endstop, so the motor is locked and the current angle becomes 0.

Homing is sensorless (stall detection on the TMC5160 drivers). Lower homing current makes stall detection more sensitive. If homing stops early in mid-air, raise the current back toward 1.0 or raise driver_SGT by 1.

VISION_HOME

Sensorless X/Y homing can land up to about 2 mm off. VISION_HOME fixes that: it runs G28 X Y, centers the down camera on datum dot 0, and sets the position to the taught coordinates. Use it instead of plain G28 before any vision work. NO_G28=1 re-references on the dot without homing first.

It runs pnpvision in the background through a gcode_shell_command. Wait for its result line in the console before moving the machine. Log: Software/pnpvision/results/vision_home_macro.log. Setup steps are on the Vision page.

Running jobs from the touchscreen

pnp_hmi.cfg lets you run pnpvision jobs from Mainsail or the touchscreen without a laptop. Install it by copying it next to printer.cfg and adding [include pnp_hmi.cfg].

  • RUN_JOB JOB=Test_PCB-top: the job name with spaces replaced by _. ALIGN=1 aligns the board first (put the down camera on alignment pad A first). NO_FEEDERS=1 means you put a part in the pocket by hand when asked.
  • Or pick a PNP_<job>.gcode launcher from the print list. The pnpvision make-launchers command creates these.
  • Questions appear as dialogs with Continue / Stop buttons.
  • The emergency stop still works and also ends the job.
  • Log: Software/pnpvision/results/run_job_macro.log.

Lights

Three LEDs on the toolhead: LED 1 shows status, LEDs 2–3 light the area under the down camera (solid white, on at boot). The up camera has a separate 6-LED ring. The LED effects need the klipper-led_effect extension.

Feeder servo

FEED moves the servo to a retract angle (20°), then an advance angle (160°), waiting 250 ms at each, then returns to rest (90°). These values are variables at the top of the macro. The servo's pulse limits are safe placeholders until it's calibrated.

For real jobs the feeders are driven by the Arduino Nano through pnpvision, not by this macro. See Vision → Feeders.

Legacy macros: PICK, PLACE, CYCLE

Don't run these on V3. They were written for V2. PICK uses a safe height of Z 80, which is outside V3's Z range of 0–60 and assumes the old Z direction. PLACE refers to an undefined default_z_place. CYCLE uses a stepper and pin that don't exist on V3. Picking and placing are now done by pnpvision.

Adding your own macros

Add a [gcode_macro NAME] section to printer.cfg with a description: and a gcode: block, then Save & Restart. Always start motion with G90 (absolute moves) and lift to a safe Z before X/Y moves. See the Klipper command templates documentation.
